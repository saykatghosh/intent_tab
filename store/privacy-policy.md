# Intent Tab — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 19, 2026_

Intent Tab is a Chrome extension that replaces the new tab page. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

## The short version
**Intent Tab does not collect, send, sell or share any of your data.** It has no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no tracking. Everything stays in your browser.

## What is stored, and where
Intent Tab saves the following **only on your computer**, in Chrome's extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`):

- your settings (theme, language, timer length, search engine, and so on)
- the intents you type, your daily goal, to-dos and notes
- simple daily counts (tabs opened, tabs with an intent, intents completed)
- custom bookmark icons and an optional background photo you choose
- cached AI suggestions and summaries

You can delete all of it at any time by removing the extension. You can also export it to a file, or import it from one, in **Settings → Your data**.

**Optional sync:** if you turn on "Sync across your Chrome browsers", your settings, to-dos, notes and projects are copied to Chrome sync (`chrome.storage.sync`), which Google handles under your Google account and Chrome's own privacy terms. Intent Tab itself still sends nothing anywhere. History and your background photo are never synced. Turning sync off stops all copying.

## Bookmarks, most visited and tabs
Intent Tab reads your bookmarks and most-visited sites **only to display them on the new tab page**, and changes bookmarks **only when you ask it to** (add, edit, rename, move, delete). It counts open tabs to show the number; it does not read tab addresses or contents.

## Focus mode
If you turn on focus mode and add sites (for example `youtube.com`), Chrome asks you to allow Intent Tab access to **those sites only**. While an intent is running, visits to those sites are redirected to a calm reminder page inside the extension. Intent Tab does not read, store or send the pages you visit.

## On-device AI
AI features use **Chrome's built-in model (Gemini Nano)**, which runs on your computer. Prompts and answers are processed locally by Chrome and are not sent to Intent Tab or to any other server by this extension. If the model is not available, simple built-in rules are used instead. You can turn AI features off in Settings.

## Search
The search bar sends your query to the search engine you choose (Google by default), the same way typing into that site would. Intent Tab does not see or keep these searches.

## Children
Intent Tab is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be published with an updated date.
